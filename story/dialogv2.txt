# Aleksej of Zaslawye — Build Document

9 beats · 8 combat encounters · 1 voiced NPC · 7 in-game documents

---

## 1. Premise

Aleksej of Zaslawye is a licensed bounty-hunter in Kuttenberg and secretly raises an army for **János Kanizsai, archbishop of Esztergom (Gran)**, on a promise of unlimited funds. Kanizsai loses, is stripped of his castle and revenues, and the money stops.

Aleksej now has an army he cannot pay and cannot safely disband. He hires Henry to liquidate it for him, paid out of the town's purse.

His officers notice which of their men keep dying, break with him, and take his original plan — storm Raborsch under Sigismund's colours — to the field without him. Henry is why the army split. Aleksej flees to the marshes with the men who came from Zaslawye.

**Beats 1–5:** Henry works for him and does not suspect.
**Beat 6:** Kuttenberg. The turn.
**Beats 7–9:** Henry hunts him.

---

## 2. Character brief — for VO

- Ruthenian, roughly fifty, in Bohemia nine years. Warm, funny, generous with money.
- **Never uses a contraction.** *Do not*, *there is*, *I will*. Not once, including the marsh.
- **Never states an enemy count** (counts scale with party size). He gives a **name** and a **place** instead.
- **"Can you imagine that?"** — his tic. Appears exactly twice (beats 3 and 4). Never in the marsh.
- Never names the lord he served until beat 9.

## 4. Beats 1–5 — implementation and dialogue

Objective text: under eight words, never omniscient, states only what Henry currently believes.

---

### Beat 1 — The camp in the woods (woodland_camp)

**Objective:** clear the camp above the wagoners' camp
**Encounter:** Leader named **Ondra**.
**Document:** none
**Trigger out:** kill everybody + return to Aleksej

#### Assignment

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
Voiceline1: You are the one they talk about in the square. Good. I was beginning to think I would have to do this myself, and I am too old and too fat for the woods.
>
2 There is a camp in the field above the wagoners' camp. The one leading them is called Ondra.
3 He took a carter's load on Thursday and took the carter's cock along with it, which was unnecessary.
4  The town council will pay two hundred groschen for justice. I will add a hundred of my own for the sake of men everywhere.

**[Henry: "Why not send the guard?"]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
5 Because the guard would march in broad daylight, in full uniform, with a bard singing of their exploits, and find cold fires and even colder latrines. You, on the other hand, know how to deal with such tasks.

#### Report

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
6   Ondra as well? Good. He was the only one of them worth worrying about.
>
7 Take the silver — take it, do not count it in front of me, it is insulting.
8You know, in the east we had a man who counted his pay in front of his lord and the whole council.
9Every week. For thirty years. And then one week he received coins still hot from the forge. He did not count anything after that.

---

### Beat 2 — The mine (mining_camp)

**Objective:** deal with the silver thieves behind the mine
**Encounter:** 9 — 6 Rabble, 3 Trained. Leader named **Vávra**.
**Document:** **Doc 1** on Vávra's body
**Trigger out:** Doc 1 in inventory + return to Aleksej

#### Assignment

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
10 Now this one is not thieves beside a road. This is thieves on a hill.
>
11 Silver has been falling through a hole in a cart's bed. Not much at a time — that is the clever part, a little and a little, and nobody's tally is wrong enough to shout about.
12  They are dug in on the height behind one of the mines. A man called Vávra keeps their count for them. He was a lumberjack before he was a thief, so he will not run. He will go down swinging something sharp and heavy.

**[Henry: "Who wants them gone?"]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
13  Everybody. The miners, their foreman, the mine's owner, the town mint, and me.

#### Report

*(Henry hands over the transfer schedule. Aleksej reads it. Hold the pause — a fraction longer than is comfortable — before the line.)*

> **ALEKSEJ:**
14  ...Well. I did not expect bandits to keep such a consistent tally of their, ehm. Earnings.
>
15 It speaks of another gaggle of them moving the silver on. *(sets it down)* You have made this my problem now, Henry. I hope you understand that. A man who knows where the silver goes and does nothing about it is an outlaw himself.

---

### Beat 3 — The convoy

**Objective:** ambush the silver convoy before Miskovitz
**Encounter:** Elite leader (hedge knight)
**Document:** none
**Trigger out:** return to Aleksej

#### Assignment

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
16 They will pass near Miskovitz, I think. Their leader is a hedge knight, and he is rumoured to have a shy sweetheart there. You would do well to take them before they reach the village.
>
17 Do not underestimate the knight, however. He has anything but the sweet disposition of his damsel. They say that when he rides with an army, they give him green boys with pitchforks and send him to soften up the enemy. He enjoys it.

**[Henry: "And the silver?"]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** 

18 Bring back what you can carry. What you cannot carry, burn it. Ah — no. It is silver. *(laughs)* Then you will simply have to break your back. Leave what is left lying in the road for the town's men to find. It does us both good to be seen to be honest.

#### Report

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
19 Oh my. That is a very large haul. Keep all of it, you have earned it.
>
20 *(after a pause)* We had a captain in the north — the far north — who paid us in salt for two winters, because there was no coin between the Dvina and God.
21 He had overheard two men in a tavern speaking of a merchant, and he misheard salt for silver, and he raided the man anyway. Two winters of salt. Can you imagine that?

**DIRECTION:** he is overpaying and he knows it. Play the generosity as slightly too fast. This is the last money he has to spend on goodwill.

---

### Beat 4 — The second camp (south_camp)

**Objective:** clear the camp at the burnt mill
**Encounter:** **Trained leader wearing Sigismund's colours**. Aleksej names the leader as **Kuneš**; the man Henry finds in charge is not Kune, some other name
**Document:** none — the surcoat is the beat
**Trigger out:** return to Aleksej

#### Assignment

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
22 Rabble have burned a mill down and then made their camp on the ashes. Can you imagine that? Sleeping on a bed of smouldering coals. It must be quite warm.
>
23  A man called Kuneš has them in hand, or thinks he does. Go and be quick — I want to eat before dark. They say eating after dark makes for bad sleep.

#### Report — the unguarded moment

**[Henry: "The one leading them wasn't called Kuneš. And he was wearing Sigismund's colours."]**

 
>
> *(no warmth at all, for the length of one breath)*
>
24 What did he wear. Exactly. Not what you think he wore.

**[Henry describes it.]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** *(warmth returns — too fast, too complete)* 

25 Hah. Well. That explains a great deal, actually.
>
26 There is a captain of Sigismund's working this country. I have heard the name twice this month and dismissed it twice, and that was stupid of me.
27 He is buying men — deserters, brigands, whatever will hold a pitchfork — and he is paying them out of somebody's silver. *(taps the table)* Somebody's silver, Henry.
>
28 It was never thieves on a hill. It was their payroll.

**DIRECTION:** the only moment in the quest where the mask is off. Cold and short — no anger, no fluster. A man going very still and asking a precise question. Everything after it is invention delivered fast, because he is assembling it as he speaks.

**BUILD NOTE:** the surcoat must be the same asset the player finds in the chest at beat 6. If those two items differ, the wordless proof at beat 6 does not land.

---

### Beat 5 — The patrol

**Objective:** intercept the captain's men on the low road
**Encounter:** 9 — Veteran + 2 Elite. A genuine Sigismund unit.
**Document:** **Doc 2** on the patrol leader
**Trigger out:** return to Aleksej

#### Assignment

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
29 I have found his men. Or they have found us — I am not certain which way it goes.
>
30 They ride the low road past the fields tomorrow. *(and here, for the first time)* **I do not know their road past that and I do not know their strength.**
31 That is the trouble. It could be nothing and it could be an army, and I would not send you if there were anyone else to send.

**[Henry: "You're sending me anyway."]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
32 I am. *(beat)* Go carefully, my friend. I have grown used to you.

#### Report — the slip

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
33 ...Bozhe. *(genuinely surprised, then covering it)* **You took them past the shrine, then**, and walked back here on your own two legs. *(laughs)* I have rarely seen a warrior of your might, my friend.

**THE SLIP — BUILD CRITICAL.** He swore he did not know their road past the fields, then named a point past the fields. He chose it. He timed the job to put Henry there.

- No sting, no objective update, no Henry reaction line. It passes at conversational speed.
- **The place he names must be the actual encounter location, and it must lie beyond the landmark given in the assignment.** These two lines are a matched pair. If the ambush site moves, both lines move. Never edit one alone.

*(Continue with no pause:)*

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
34 Sit. Sit. I am going to tell you something I should have told you at the very beginning of our acquaintance, and you may be angry with me for it.
>
35 There is no captain. There is a man of God — a Hungarian. Kanizsai, archbishop of Gran, or he was. He dropped a crown on the wrong bald head last summer, and Sigismund took his castle, his income and his dignity, in that order.
36 Not his wife, mind. Being a churchman, he had none. It is the only thing left to him.
>
37 What silver he still has, he sends north. Here. To buy an army out of other men's mines — parts of which you have been turning into red stains on our fields.

**[Henry: "Why tell me now?"]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
38 Because you keep coming back alive, and a man who keeps coming back alive eventually finds things out for himself. I would rather he found them out from a friend.

**DIRECTION:** every word of this is true. He omits one clause — he is the man collecting. Play it as relief, not confession.

## 5. Beats 6–8 — no dialogue

Aleksej does not speak again until the marsh. Do not add a note, a read-aloud letter, or a parting line on his door., he just disappears from the kuttenberg in he resides in.

---

### Beat 6 — Kuttenberg

**Objective in:** confront Aleksej in Kuttenberg
**Objective out:** the transition here is the reveal, and it is the most important text in the quest. Every player reads it; almost none will read Docs 3 and 4.

**Requirements:**
- A lockable, dressable interior for Aleksej. Without it the reveal falls back onto letters.
- **A chest containing Sigismund surcoats** — same asset as the beat 4 leader. This is the proof. No text.
- **Doc 3** and **Doc 4** placed in the same room.
- Aleksej absent. Not a scripted flight — he left while Henry rode back from the patrol.
- No combat.

---

**Objective:** find the men gathering at the old fort
**Encounter:** **Document:** **Doc 5**
**Trigger out:** Doc 5 in inventory

Henry is late because he went to Kuttenberg. The empty fort states this; nothing needs to say it.

## 6. Beat 9 — The marsh

**Objective:** find Aleksej in the marshes
**Encounter:**the Ruthenians. Give them gear that matches nothing else in the quest.
**Document:** **Doc 7** on his body.

> **ALEKSEJ:** 

39 Stop there. Not because of my men — they will not move unless I tell them. Stop there because I want to say this once, and I will not shout it.
>
40  This water smells of home. Did you know that? Same reeds, same rot, same flat grey sky sitting on it like a lid. Nine years in this country, and I find Zaslawye in a bog outside Kuttenberg. *(a short laugh)* God is not subtle.

**[Henry: "Who were you?"]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
41 I served a man. I have not said his name aloud since I crossed the Vistula, and I am going to say it now, because after today there is nobody left who could be hurt by it.
>
> Švitrigaila.
>
42  *(beat)* You have never heard of him. That is the joke. I gave him everything I had, and you have never heard of him.

**[Henry: "And your men?"]**

> **ALEKSEJ:** 
43They came with me from Zaslawye. Not for the archbishop — they have never heard of him either, and they pray to a different God in any case. They came because I told them there was better land in the west. I was right. The land here is very good.
>
44 They cannot go home. Neither can I. So they will die in a marsh for a churchman who stopped writing to me in the autumn, and they will not complain about it, because they never do.
>
45 *(drawing)* Do not make us wait, Henry. It is unkind.

**If pre-assault dialogue is not possible**, in order of preference:
1. Hook it to the duel trigger — after his men are down, before the duel begins.
2. Break it into three or four combat barks fired as the fight opens.
3. Cut it and put only the name *Švitrigaila* on his body.

Do not move it to a document.


## 8. The documents

Rules: one fact each, payload in the first sentence, sixty words maximum. No document carries a beat that is not already covered by dialogue, an object, or a fight.

---

### Doc 1 — *A thief's tally*
**Beat 2 · on Vávra's body**

> Fourth load goes out Wednesday next, by the Miskovitz road, before the bell.
>
> Same cart, same driver, same story if anyone stops him. Weigh it at both ends this time. The last was light by a mark and a half and Vávra swears on his mother it was not us, which means it was us.
>
> Burn this.

---

### Doc 2 — *A patrol captain's report*
**Beat 5 · on the patrol leader**

> There are men gathering at the old Roman fort. More each week, and not the sort who gather for the harvest.
>
> We have swept the low road twice and found only burnt camps and fresh graves. Someone is doing our work for us and I do not like it. Send more men or send orders. Do not send neither.

---

### Doc 3 — *From the archbishop's chancery*
**Beat 6 · Aleksej's lodging**

> To Aleksej of Zaslawye —
>
> You shall have coin for as many men as you can put under arms, without limit set, as was agreed at Trnava. Spend as you must and account for it afterward.
>
> His Grace asks one thing only: that it be finished before the snow.

---

### Doc 4 — *The last letter*
**Beat 6 · Aleksej's lodging**

> There is no more money.
>
> His Grace's revenues are administered out of Esztergom by the lord Stiborius and will remain so until the King is pleased otherwise. You are advised to release the men you have raised and remove yourself from that country.
>
> This is the last you will have from us.

---

### Doc 5 — *Orders for the muster*
**Beat 7 · Roman fort**

> Raborsch. The walls are old and the garrison is thin.
>
> We take it, we hold it, and we winter behind it — and what we cannot spend we can coin there, which is more than can be said for a hole in a hillside.
>
> March in Sigismund's colours. Let the King's men answer for what follows.

---

### Doc 6 — *The officers' break*
**Beat 8 · on the last officer**

> We are done with him.
>
> Count the dead since spring. Every man he sent to the woods. Every man who asked twice about his pay. Not one of his own household. He has been selling us to a bounty-hunter a camp at a time and calling it bad luck.
>
> We take Raborsch ourselves. We take it now.

---

### Doc 7 — *Unsent*
**Beat 9 · on Aleksej's body**

> Your Grace — I have your letter.
>
> I did not release the men. There was nowhere for them to go and you knew that when you wrote to me.
>
> I do not ask for money now. I ask you to write once more, so that they may see a seal and believe there was ever anything behind it.
